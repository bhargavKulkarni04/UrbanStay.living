Notice board

Never miss a water cut or a gate-time change.

Every notice from your PG lands on one board, newest first, with live alerts pinned on top. Tank cleaning, Wi-Fi maintenance, gate timings, rent reminders — all in one place instead of buried in a group chat.

  • Live alerts highlighted at the top
  • Every notice time-stamped, so you know what's current
  • A notification on your phone the moment it's posted
Mess menu

Know what's for dinner before you get hungry.

The whole two-week mess menu, day by day. Open the app and today's breakfast, lunch and dinner are right there — no more asking the kitchen what's cooking.

  • Today's menu opens first
  • Switch between Week 1 and Week 2
  • Breakfast, lunch and dinner on one screen
Laundry booking

Book the washing machine, skip the queue.

See which machines are free, which one is running and when it frees up. Pick a day, book a slot, and your PG's fair-use rules apply to everyone — so nobody hogs a machine all evening.

  • Live status for every machine
  • Pick a day and book a slot in seconds
  • All your bookings in one tab
Move-out notice

Move out without the deposit drama.

Pick your vacating date and the app counts the notice period for you. Your deposit, any electricity or repair deductions and the final refund are laid out line by line before you submit — no surprises on your last day.

  • Every deduction shown before you submit
  • Refund sent to the UPI ID you choose
