## 5. Core Product Deep-Dives (8 Functional Modules)

### Module 1: 0% Direct Bank UPI Collection
* **Headline**: *"Your Money. Your Bank Account. Zero Gateway Cut."*
* **Detailed Copy**:
  Most software aggregators act as middlemen, holding rent for 2 to 3 days and skimming a 1.8% to 2.5% transaction fee. With UrbanStay, you plug in your own bank UPI ID (`owner@hdfcbank` or `owner@sbi`). When a resident pays via Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm, the funds settle into your personal account in real-time.
* **Key Bullets**:
  * Dynamic, tenant-specific UPI QR codes embedded with exact monthly rent dues.
  * UTR transaction verification with automated screenshot audit.
  * Automatic payment status toggle from "Due" to "Paid" with zero manual data entry.

---

### Module 2: 50ms Offline HRA-Stamped PDF Rent Receipts
* **Headline**: *"Lightning-Fast Receipts. Works Even in Basement Rooms."*
* **Detailed Copy**:
  Bengaluru and Kota PGs frequently suffer from weak cell coverage in ground and basement floors. Cloud-heavy apps spin endlessly or fail. UrbanStay's native engine renders professional, tax-compliant PDF rent receipts in 50 milliseconds locally on your device without waiting for the internet.
* **Key Bullets**:
  * Itemized breakdown: Room Rent + Electricity Units + Wi-Fi/Maintenance charges.
  * Landlord PAN and official revenue stamp for tenant corporate HRA tax claims.
  * 1-tap dispatch directly to the tenant's WhatsApp and email inbox.

---

### Module 3: Zero-App Reception Desk QR Standee Check-In
* **Headline**: *"Check-In New Tenants in 90 Seconds. Zero Paperwork."*
* **Detailed Copy**:
  Place the UrbanStay branded QR Standee at your PG entrance or reception desk. When a new tenant arrives with their luggage, they scan the QR code with their default smartphone camera. They don't have to download any app from the Play Store.
* **Key Bullets**:
  * Instant mobile web onboarding form ([tenant_checkin.html](file:///c:/Users/bharg/OneDrive/Desktop/UrbanStay/ProductionCode/tenant_checkin.html)).
  * Live camera selfie capture and front/back Aadhaar card upload.
  * Digital house rules sign-off with timestamped emergency contact verification.
  * Instant room keycard and digital gate pass generated on their browser.

### Module 4: 30-Day Vacant Bed Pipeline & Notice Tracker
* **Headline**: *"Zero Empty Beds. Maximize Every Square Foot."*

## 10. Comprehensive FAQ Accordion (10 Real Landlord Questions)

#### 1. Does UrbanStay take any percentage cut or commission from my tenant's rent?
**Answer**: Absolutely 0%. We are a pure software operating system, not a payment gateway. When your residents pay rent through UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), the money goes directly into your personal or current bank account with ₹0 gateway commission.

#### 2. Do my tenants need to download an app to check in or pay rent?
**Answer**: No! Residents can simply scan the UrbanStay Reception QR standee on their smartphone camera. They can complete their check-in, submit Aadhaar verification, pay rent, or download HRA receipts directly in their mobile browser without downloading any software. A zero-lag mobile app is also available for those who prefer it.

#### 3. What happens if our PG has weak mobile network or the Wi-Fi is down?
**Answer**: UrbanStay is engineered offline-first. PDF rent receipts render in 50 milliseconds directly on your device without relying on internet servers. Any collections or entries you make offline sync automatically as soon as connectivity resumes.

#### 4. Can I prevent my warden or ground staff from pocketing cash collections?
**Answer**: Yes. UrbanStay features an automated Anti-Pocketing Cash Audit. The moment staff logs a cash collection on the dashboard, an automated verification SMS and WhatsApp receipt is dispatched to the tenant. If a warden takes cash without logging it, the tenant receives no receipt, triggering an instant audit discrepancy.

#### 5. How does the electricity sub-meter billing work?
**Answer**: In shared rooms with ACs or individual sub-meters, you simply enter the initial and final meter readings. UrbanStay automatically multiplies the consumed units by your PG's electricity rate per unit, splits the cost across room tenants, and attaches it directly to the monthly rent invoice.

#### 6. How does the food mess headcount save grocery costs?
**Answer**: Residents tap their daily meal choices (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner) on their portal by a designated cutoff time. Your cook receives an accurate headcount on their phone, eliminating guesswork and preventing thousands of rupees in wasted milk, vegetables, and LPG.

#### 7. How long does it take to migrate from our current Excel sheets or registers?
**Answer**: Less than 7 minutes. Our step-by-step setup wizard allows you to add your property floors, rooms, and sharing types easily. If you have an existing tenant list, our onboarding team can import your entire Excel register for free.

#### 8. Are the digital rent receipts accepted by IT companies for HRA tax exemption?
**Answer**: Yes. Every UrbanStay rent receipt includes the tenant's details, property address, owner's PAN number, rental period, itemized breakdown, and an official digital revenue stamp compliant with Indian Income Tax HRA guidelines.

#### 9. Can I manage multiple PG buildings in different locations?
**Answer**: Yes. Our Growth and Multi-Property tiers allow you to switch seamlessly between different buildings, assign separate wardens to each property, and view consolidated monthly income statements on a single screen.

#### 10. Can I get a GST invoice for my UrbanStay subscription?
**Answer**: Yes. UrbanStay Technologies Pvt. Ltd. provides complete tax-compliant B2B invoices with 18% GST itemized under SAC Code 998315 so you can claim full Input Tax Credit (ITC).
