## 💰 7. INDIVIDUAL TENANT LEASE RATE LOCKING & GRANDFATHERED TARIFF ARCHITECTURE

### The Real-World Domain Problem:
In Indian PGs, older tenants who moved in 1 year ago often pay **₹9,000/month** for a 2-sharing room, while new incoming tenants are charged **₹9,500/month**. When the owner updates the global property tariffs in Settings, it **MUST NEVER** accidentally inflate or mutate existing tenants' contracted rent.

### The 3-Tier Architectural Rule:
1. **Per-Tenant Lease Contract Rate Locking (`tenant.contracted_monthly_rent`)**:
   * When a tenant is onboarded, their agreed monthly rent (e.g. ₹9,000) is immutably stamped to their individual lease record in PostgreSQL.
   * Monthly rent invoices, WhatsApp dues chasers, and PDF receipts strictly pull this locked rate.
2. **Global Property Tariff Update (Future Check-Ins Only)**:
   * Updating base tariffs in **Settings ➔ Sharing Rents** (e.g. 2-sharing $\rightarrow$ ₹9,500) sets the default rate strictly for **NEW check-ins and vacant bed listings**.
   * It never touches active resident accounts.
3. **Explicit Individual Rent Revision (Owner Override)**:
   * If an owner wishes to increase rent for an existing tenant (e.g. upon 11-month agreement renewal), they must explicitly open that specific resident's profile in **Rooms & Beds** and perform an explicit manual rent revision with confirmation.

## 🌦️ 8. SMART WEATHER API: AUTOMATED MONSOON RAIN ALERT FOR TERRACE CLOTHES DRYING

### The Real-World Domain Problem:
In Indian tech hubs like Bengaluru (BTM Layout, HSR, Koramangala), sudden 4 PM downpours are common during monsoon season. PG residents hang their washed laundry on rooftop drying lines and head to office or stay in their rooms, returning to find completely soaked clothes. 
Currently, wardens have to run upstairs in the rain or owners get bombarded with complaints.

### The Automated Zero-Work Architecture:
1. **Hyperlocal Weather API Integration**:
   - Integrated with **Open-Meteo API** (100% free, open-source, zero API-key overhead) or **WeatherAPI** (1M free requests/month).
   - Backend or client scheduler polls hyperlocal precipitation probabilities for the PG's exact GPS coordinates/pincode (e.g. 12.9166° N, 77.6101° E for BTM Layout) every 30 minutes between 8:00 AM and 8:00 PM.
2. **Predictive Rain Trigger (> 70% Precipitation Probability)**:
   - When rain is predicted within the next 30–45 minutes:
     - Triggers high-priority push notification to all active residents:
       > 🌧️ **Terrace Rain Alert — UrbanStay PG**  
       > *Rain is expected in the next 30 minutes! If you have clothes drying on the terrace, please collect them now.*
     - Dynamic visual badge appears on the **Facilities & Amenities** dashboard card:
       * 🌧️ `Rain Alert: Rain expected in ~25m — Collect Terrace Clothes` (vs ☀️ `Clear skies — Safe to dry clothes`).
3. **The Business & Operational Win**:
   - **Zero Owner/Warden Work**: Completely automated background process. No warden running to the terrace or shouting in WhatsApp groups.
   - **High Resident Delight**: Saves tenant clothes from being ruined, driving organic word-of-mouth adoption and high app retention.

## 💳 9. AUTOMATED PAYMENT RECONCILIATION EVOLUTION & DUAL-MODE ARCHITECTURE (RBI COMPLIANT)

### The Strategic Context:
Indian PG rent transactions involve high-ticket monthly amounts (₹8,000 to ₹18,000 per tenant). Standard payment gateways charge **1.95% to 2% + 18% GST** (₹180 to ₹400 per bed/month), which cuts deep into thin hostel operating margins. Real PG owners refuse to absorb this cost, and tenants will not pay convenience surcharges on rent.

### Phase A: Current Implemented Phase — Direct 0% P2P UPI Intent (`upi://pay`)
* **How it Operates**:
  * In the tenant app checkout (`RentPaymentScreen`), tapping any UPI provider (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) fires an Android native deep-link intent:
    `upi://pay?pa=<owner_upi_id>&pn=<owner_name>&am=<rent_amount>&cu=INR&tn=UrbanStay_Rent_<room>&tr=<txn_ref>`
  * The tenant completes the transfer directly in their personal banking/UPI app.
  * The tenant provides or auto-captures the 12-digit Bank Reference Number (UTR).
  * The owner receives the rent directly into their personal bank account (HDFC, SBI, ICICI, etc.) with **0% gateway cut**.
  * The owner opens **`owner_approvals.html` (Screen 7)** and taps **"Approve & Send Receipt"** after checking their bank SMS/statement.
* **Why This Was Built First (The Strategic Moat)**:
  1. **₹0.00 Transaction Fee**: Maximum margin retention for both owner and tenant.
  2. **Zero Owner KYC Friction**: The PG owner does NOT need to register for any payment gateway, submit commercial KYC, or upload business PAN/GSTIN. They can onboard in 30 seconds by simply entering their UPI ID.
  3. **Zero Regulatory Risk**: UrbanStay acts purely as a software utility. Money never touches platform corporate nodes, fully complying with RBI Payment Aggregator (PA/PG) guidelines without intermediary license overhead.

---

### Phase B: Future Integration Phase — Automated Sub-Merchant Virtual Accounts (Cashfree AutoCollect / Razorpay Route)

As UrbanStay scales to multi-property chains and high-volume operators who demand 100% hands-free bank reconciliation without manual UTR checking:

1. **Sub-Merchant Onboarding Engine (RBI Compliant)**:
   * Under RBI rules, a central SaaS platform cannot collect rent into its own pool and redistribute funds without Payment Aggregator compliance.
   * UrbanStay will integrate **Cashfree Sub-Subscriptions / Marketplace Split** or **Razorpay Route**.
   * For enterprise owners who want automated status callbacks, UrbanStay provides a seamless in-app **Sub-Merchant Verification Flow** where the owner submits PAN and Bank details directly to the gateway's verified compliance engine.
2. **Dedicated Virtual VPA / Bed Bank Account**:
   * Once approved, the gateway assigns a dedicated Virtual UPI ID per bed/tenant (e.g. `urbanstay.arunpg.bed104@icici`).
   * When the tenant pays rent via any UPI app to this virtual ID, the money is instantly and automatically settled straight into the owner's linked bank account.
3. **Flat Low-Fee Economics (₹2 to ₹5 per Transaction)**:
   * Instead of a 2% percentage cut (which would be ₹200 on ₹10,000 rent), Virtual Account Collections utilize a **flat fee structure (₹2.00 to ₹5.00 per transaction + GST)**.
   * This negligible flat cost can be absorbed by the SaaS plan or billed as a micro-line item.
4. **Instant Webhook Reconciliation (Zero-Lag)**:
   * The payment gateway triggers a real-time secure webhook (`payment.captured` / `virtual_account.credited`) to the UrbanStay backend.
   * Status updates instantly from `PENDING` to `PAID` in milliseconds.
   * Digital HRA-compliant PDF rent receipt is automatically generated and dispatched via WhatsApp Cloud API without any manual human intervention.
